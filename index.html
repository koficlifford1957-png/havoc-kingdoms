Havoc Kingdoms

Break the crown. Burn the kingdom.

How to play1. Draft 4 units and pick an arena.
2. Fortify: tap your bricks to add armor around your gold keep.
3. Battle: pull back anywhere and release to fire. Each unit has its own reload.
4. Break the enemy’s gold keep to win and climb from Squire to King.
